    Methodology & caveats — please read

    Assessed values are real. Parcel boundaries, APNs, addresses, land/improvement values, exemptions and net assessed values come from the Alameda County Assessor's official parcel service (snapshot verified Nov 1, 2026: 24,140 Dublin parcels, $25,083,551,342 total net assessed value). The parcel layer on this map reads that county service live, so drill-down values stay current. Values are Proposition 13 roll values (purchase price + max 2%/yr), not market values.

    Tax figures are estimates, labeled as such. There is no public bulk file of actual taxes paid per parcel — the county releases bills one parcel at a time. Estimated tax = net assessed value × tax rate. The low end uses the official FY2025-26 countywide base levy of 1.0000%. The high end uses the California Board of Equalization's published Alameda County average rate of 1.220%. Each TRA's true composite rate (1% + voter-approved bonds for that exact area) sits in the county's "51-427 Composite Tax Rates" report; that PDF is hosted behind a county SharePoint login wall and could not be retrieved programmatically — when those 43 rates are loaded, this map will show exact per-TRA estimates instead of the range.

    Estimates exclude fixed direct charges, special assessments, and Mello-Roos community-facilities-district charges, which are billed per parcel and can add thousands of dollars a year in newer east Dublin developments. 2,273 Dublin parcels (9.4%) carry a $0 net value — mostly common areas and exempt parcels — and pay no ad valorem tax. 135 APNs appear on more than one parcel row (condos/multi-unit), so drill-down is keyed to the individual parcel, not the APN alone.

    Actual bills, one click from every parcel. Selecting a parcel now shows a "View actual tax bill at the county" button. The Alameda County Treasurer-Tax Collector portal does not publish direct links to individual bills — verified Nov 1, 2026: its bill page address carries no parcel identifier (the portal selects the bill from your search session — even the older-style bill addresses with a tracer number in them are ignored, verified the same day) and its search page ignores an APN passed in the web address — so the button copies the parcel's APN and opens the county's official search; choose the Parcel Number tab, paste, pick the suggestion, and use "View Bill" on the summary. The gap this matters for is real: one Tassajara Rd parcel (APN 985-133-5, net assessed $701,148) shows an estimate of $7,011–$8,554 on this map, but its actual 2026-27 county bill is $16,342.56 — a 1.2331% composite rate plus $7,696.72 in fixed charges, mostly CFD No. 2024-1 (Mello-Roos) charges that no assessed-value estimate can see.
